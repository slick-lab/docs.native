# Welcome to native.cr

## A New Way to Build Mobile Apps

native.cr is a framework that brings the Crystal programming language to mobile development. Write your app logic once in Crystal. Deploy to Android. Run on desktop during development. iOS support is on the horizon.

## Why Another Framework?

We love Crystal. We love its speed, its clarity, its type system. But every time we wanted to build a mobile app, we had to leave Crystal behind and reach for JavaScript, Dart, or Kotlin.

We built native.cr because we believe Crystal deserves a place on mobile devices. Not through a bridge. Not through an interpreter. Direct compilation to native ARM64 code. Just Crystal talking to the metal.

## What We Have Built

- A complete UI framework with 24 modules
- Android engine with OpenGL ES rendering
- Desktop preview with SDL2
- State preservation across fast restarts
- CLI tools for creating, building, and debugging
- Support for camera, audio, storage, networking, gestures, animations, and more

native.cr is young. Version 0.1 is just the beginning. We have plans for:
